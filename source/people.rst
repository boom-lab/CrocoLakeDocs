About
======

CrocoLake is developed at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI) and is supported by the National Science Foundation under Grant No. 2311383, “Collaborative Research: Frameworks: A community platform for accelerating observationally-constrained regional oceanographic modeling”.

If you would like to contribute, or have any feedback, please reach out to any of us!

Team
^^^^^^^^^

Enrico Milanese
---------------

*Data Scientist, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

Email: enrico.milanese@whoi.edu

Enrico Milanese is a data scientist on the NSF CSSI project CROCODILE, a collaboration with NCAR to build software infrastructure for developing and accessing regional ocean model configurations. His work focuses on bringing physical and biogeochemical ocean observations from different sources (floats, gliders, bottle data and others) into regional models of the northwest Atlantic. CrocoLake has been his main project since joining WHOI in 2024. He developed his research software expertise through studies and research in computational mechanics at the University of Padova (Italy), EPFL (Switzerland) and MIT (USA).

David (Roo) Nicholson
---------------------

*Associate Scientist, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

Email: dnicholson@whoi.edu

Dr. Nicholson's research uses autonomous platforms such as floats and gliders as well as numerical models to understand the interplay of physics and biology in the global cycling of carbon, oxygen and other elements. He has extensive experience studying dissolved gas dynamics in the ocean, ranging from greenhouse gas fluxes, to stable oxygen isotopic productivity tracers to passive noble gas tracers of air-sea exchange processes. He leads the BOOM Lab at WHOI.

Susan Wijffels
--------------

*Senior Scientist, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

Email: swijffels@whoi.edu

Susan aims to quantify and understand the role of the ocean in climate, key aspects of the large-scale ocean circulation and global ocean change. She also is recognised for her contributions to the design, implementation and exploitation of the Global Ocean Observing System (GOOS). Susan received a B.Sc. Hons (First Class) from the Flinders University of South Australia, in 1986, and a PhD in 1993 from the MIT/WHOI Joint Program in Oceanography and Oceanographic Engineering. She was with the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) Marine Laboratory in Australia from 1994, becoming a principle senior researcher, before joining WHOI in July 2017.

Susan is a Senior Scientist who has interests in the dynamics of the general circulation, climate drivers of ocean change, and understanding the Indonesian Throughflow. She has worked on various elements of the global ocean observing system, helps lead the WHOI Argo effort and co-chairs the international Argo Steering Team.
