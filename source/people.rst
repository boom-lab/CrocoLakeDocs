Enrico Milanese
---------------

*Research Associate, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

Enrico Milanese is a data scientist on the NSF CSSI project CROCODILE, a collaboration with NCAR to build software infrastructure for developing and accessing regional ocean model configurations. His work focuses on bringing physical and biogeochemical ocean observations from different sources (floats, gliders, bottle data and others) into regional models of the northwest Atlantic. He developed his research software expertise through studies and research in computational mechanics at the University of Padova (Italy), EPFL (Switzerland) and MIT (USA).

Susan Wijffels
--------------

*Senior Scientist, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

Susan Wijffels works on quantifying the ocean's role in climate, large-scale ocean circulation and global ocean change, and is known for work on the design, implementation and use of the Global Ocean Observing System (GOOS). Research interests include general circulation dynamics, climate drivers of ocean change and the Indonesian Throughflow. Susan Wijffels helps lead the WHOI Argo effort and co-chairs the international Argo Steering Team.

Susan Wijffels received a B.Sc. (Hons, First Class) from Flinders University of South Australia in 1986 and a PhD from the MIT/WHOI Joint Program in 1993. From 1994 Susan Wijffels worked at the CSIRO Marine Laboratory in Australia, becoming a principal senior researcher, and joined WHOI in July 2017.

David (Roo) Nicholson
---------------------

*Associate Scientist, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution*

David Nicholson's research uses autonomous platforms such as floats and gliders, together with numerical models, to study how physics and biology interact in the global cycling of carbon, oxygen and other elements. This work draws on extensive experience studying dissolved gas dynamics in the ocean, including greenhouse gas fluxes, stable oxygen isotope tracers of productivity, and noble gas tracers of air-sea exchange. David Nicholson leads the BOOM Lab at WHOI.
